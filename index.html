User Guide

Master your Second Brain Pro with this complete feature overview.

📊

Insight Dashboard

Track your productivity with real-time stats. See your total note count, discover your most used tags, and get personalized insights into your focus areas.

⭐

Priority System

Stay organized using the Star icon for important tasks and the Check icon for completed ones. Important notes always appear at the top.

🔗

Smart Connections

The app automatically finds related notes by analyzing your keywords. Discover hidden links between your thoughts as you write.

🎯

Focus Mode

Click the target icon on any note card to enter a distraction-free environment. Set your own time limit and use the looping alarm to stay on track.

✍️

Daily Reflections

End your day by capturing what you've learned. Use the AI Summarizer to create quick TL;DR versions of your thoughts for easy review.

💾

Privacy First

All your data is stored locally in your browser's Local Storage. No clouds, no servers—your thoughts stay strictly on your device.

📈

Weekly Performance

Check the dashboard for your Weekly Report. Track your completion rate and see which topics dominated your week.

🔍

Search & Filters

Find anything instantly using the search bar. You can also click on any tag in the sidebar to filter your entire collection by that topic.

🌗

Theme Modes

Switch between Light and Dark modes at any time using the sun/moon icon. Your preference is saved automatically.
